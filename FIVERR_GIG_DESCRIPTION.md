---

### 🌟 What You Get in This System:

#### 1. 🛎️ Front Desk Receptionist Visual Room Rack
- **Interactive Color-Coded Room Matrix**: Instant status indicators for Available 🟢, Occupied 🔴, Maintenance 🟡, and Reserved 🔵.
- **Real-Time Booking Synchronization**: Any booking made online by a customer instantly marks the room as occupied on the receptionist’s screen.
- **Dynamic Date-Based Availability**: Changing check-in/out dates recalculates room status on the fly.
- **1-Click Walk-In Booking**: Register walk-in guests, collect payments, and assign rooms on the spot.
- **Double-Booking Prevention**: Database-level locking guarantees zero overlapping reservation conflicts (`409 Conflict`).

#### 2. 🌐 Public Guest Online Booking Engine
- Dynamic room search with live date pickers, price range sliders, guest counters, and amenity filters.
- Detailed room showcase pages with photo galleries, specifications, and house rules.
- **4-Step Booking Wizard**: Guest details, stay breakdown, multi-channel payment UI (Card, Bank Transfer, Cash on Arrival, Mobile Wallets).
- **Automated Booking Confirmation Folios**: Printable PDF receipts with unique reservation voucher numbers (`GH-2026-XXXX`).

#### 3. 📊 Executive Admin Dashboard & Financial Reports
- KPI Command Center: Total Rooms, Occupancy Rates, Today's Check-ins, and Gross Sales.
- Interactive SVG Revenue Analytics & Booking Volume charts.
- **Audited Financial Statements**: Daily, Weekly, and Monthly reports with Average Daily Rate (ADR), RevPAR, and paid vs pending receivables.
- **1-Click Export to CSV (Excel)** & Printable Tax Invoices.
- Guest CRM, Payment Transaction Ledger, and Staff Roster Management.

#### 4. 🤖 AI Telegram Reporting Bot
- Monitor hotel operations 24/7 directly from your phone via Telegram!
- **Natural Language Understanding (Roman Urdu & English)**:
  - *"aaj ki report"*, *"aaj ka hisab"*, *"today revenue"* ➔ Instant Daily Business Report
  - *"is hafte ki report"*, *"hafte ka hisab"* ➔ 7-Day Financial Summary
  - *"is mahine ka hisab"* ➔ Monthly Revenue & Folio Report
  - *"kamray"*, *"kitne rooms khali hain"* ➔ Live Room Status Matrix
- Slash commands (`/today`, `/week`, `/month`, `/rooms`, `/summary`) + custom 1-tap mobile reply buttons.
- Auto-reconnect daemon with proxy support for restricted regional networks.

- **Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Bootstrap 5, Bootstrap Icons, Axios, Formik + Yup
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, TypeScript (`tsc`), JWT Auth, bcryptjs
- **Database**: MySQL 8.4 (`mysql2` connection pool)
- **Bot Engine**: Telegram Bot API, Native Fetch Long Polling
